GALICIA · RIBADAVIA · O RIBEIRO · A ARNOIA

A place to leave the rush behind.

Among vineyards, beside an oak woodland, with the Miño woven into the landscape. Casa Cruceira is in Lapela, a small village in A Arnoia, a place from which to discover O Ribeiro at your own pace.

YOUR VILLAGE

Lapela: the pleasure of simple things.

Your escape begins as you open the door. Stone, vines and village paths, with the shade of the oak woodland close by and a way of life that invites you to slow down. A small place to set off walking, listen and enjoy without filling your diary.

RIVERS, VINES AND VILLAGE LIFE

A Arnoia, beside the water.

The Arnoia and Miño rivers shape our local landscape. Riverside walks, thermal traditions and regional flavours mean you do not have to go far to enjoy yourselves. Head down to the river, try the local food and leave time for an unhurried stop.

A TOWN TO EXPLORE ON FOOT

Ribadavia: stories among stone streets.

Nearby Ribadavia awaits. Explore the historic centre, discover the legacy of its Jewish quarter and visit the castle before finding a terrace for a drink. It is one of those places best enjoyed without a fixed itinerary, letting one street lead you to the next.

A LANDSCAPE TO SAVOUR

O Ribeiro, much more than wine.

Vineyards, wineries, villages and riverside landscapes shape a region with an identity of its own. Wine is a wonderful introduction, but not the only one: there are paths, heritage, food and quiet corners simply to pause in. Casa Cruceira can be your starting point and the place you look forward to returning to.

Before you arrive

How many guests can stay?

Up to 4 guests. There are two bedrooms, each with a 135 cm-wide double bed, one bathroom and a cot available.

Are the grounds private?

Yes. The 500 m² outdoor space is exclusively for Casa Cruceira guests during their stay.

Can we bring our pet?

Pets are welcome. Please keep them off beds and sofas and keep the outdoor space clean.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in from 3 pm and check-out before 11 am. You will receive access instructions before arrival.
