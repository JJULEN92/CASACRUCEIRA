STONE, VINES AND PEOPLE

Before it was a home, it was a winery.

La Cruceira’s story began with wine. Traces of the old stone winery remain part of the property’s identity.

Manuel brought it back to life, restored its vines and continued making wine for decades. A period of silence followed, until Julián and Lua fell in love with the place and began restoring it.

Today we care for what made us stay: the stone, the wood, the imperfect corners and that feeling of calm on arrival. We want to share it with those who love places with a story.

Today, you are part of this story.
