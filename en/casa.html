AT YOUR DISPOSAL

Everything for an enjoyable stay.

A place to work remotely, too

Wifi and a table for your laptop, with space to concentrate when you need to. Then it is time to slow down again.

Wifi and a place to work
A table for your laptop and wifi available whenever you need to connect.
A kitchen to enjoy
A traditional wood-burning cooker, ceramic hob, oven, microwave and fridge. Prepare whatever you fancy, at your own pace.
Everyday comforts
A dishwasher, washing machine and heating for a comfortable stay throughout the year.
Enjoy the grounds
500 m² exclusively for you, an outdoor table, a bench with a view and a hanging chair to unwind in.
Barbecue and fire pit
A stone barbecue and a fire pit area for spending time together, whenever conditions and fire-prevention restrictions permit their use.
Freshly picked fruit
If you find ripe fruit on the trees, you are welcome to pick what you will eat during your stay. Take care of the branches and leave some for the next guests.
Coffee, books and games
A coffee and tea corner, books and board games. Little reasons to slow down.
Welcome too
A cot is available and pets are welcome. Please keep pets off beds and sofas and keep the grounds clean.

If you use the traditional wood-burning cooker, please follow the lighting and safe-use instructions provided in the house.
